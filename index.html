Science, accuracy, and limitations.

Calibration matters

Sensor readings depend on proper calibration and use. Follow the device's final instructions for preparing, calibrating, and cleaning each sensor.

Readings are estimates

Placement, pressure, temperature, products, and sensor condition can affect results. A single reading cannot determine skin type or diagnose a skin condition.

Use as educational context

Consider the readings as general information about the prototype, not as medical advice or a replacement for a qualified professional.

Prototype note

Built to explore, not diagnose.

DermaSense is a prototype. Its measurement methods and skin-contact instructions should be validated before being presented as consumer or clinical guidance.
