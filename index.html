How to use DermaSense

Measure, review, and care.

Follow the instructions for your specific device version.

Step 01

Prepare

Power on the device and prepare the sensors as instructed.

Step 02

Measure moisture

Use the sensor only with the contact method described in the guide.

Step 03

Check pH and oil

Follow the validated pH-probe steps and use fresh blotting paper.

Step 04

Review and clean

Treat readings as estimates, then clean and store the device.

Important

Use the validated device instructions.

DermaSense is a prototype for educational use, not a medical diagnostic device. Do not use a probe directly on skin unless that use has been validated for your device.

Transparent by design

Science, accuracy, and limitations.

Calibration matters

Sensor readings depend on proper calibration and use. Follow the device instructions for preparing, calibrating, and cleaning each sensor.

Readings are estimates

Placement, pressure, temperature, products, and sensor condition can affect results. A single reading cannot determine skin type or diagnose a skin condition.

Use as educational context

Consider readings as general information about this prototype, not as medical advice or a replacement for a qualified professional.

Prototype note

Built to explore, not diagnose.

Validate the measurement methods and skin-contact instructions before presenting them as consumer or clinical guidance.
