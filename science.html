Science & Accuracy.

SkinSense explains how the device works, how it is calibrated, and where its limits are.

Why it matters

Why these readings matter.

Moisture

Hydration affects comfort, texture, and the appearance of dryness.

pH

Skin pH can provide useful context about the skin barrier and cleansing routine.

Oiliness

Surface oil can help identify possible skin type patterns.

Step by step

Calibration process.

  • Moisture: compare dry and wet skin references.
  • pH: use buffer references around pH 4.0 and 9.0.
  • Oiliness: compare clean paper with oil-saturated paper.

Our limitations

DermaSense is an educational prototype and should not be treated as a clinical diagnostic device.

Readings may vary depending on pressure, placement, temperature, skin condition, and calibration quality.

DermaSense does not replace professional dermatological advice.

Prototype note
