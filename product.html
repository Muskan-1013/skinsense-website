The SkinSense device

Meet DermaSense.

Small device. Clearer skin insights.

DermaSense combines simple sensors with practical skincare education to help you understand your skin.

What it measures

Four useful insights.

Each reading is an estimate designed for learning about the prototype — not a clinical conclusion.

  • Moisture — is your skin asking for hydration?
  • pH — is your barrier sitting in a balanced range?
  • Oiliness — how much surface oil is present?
  • Ingredient guidance — educational suggestions to explore.

Moisture
