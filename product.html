The SkinSense device

Meet DermaSense.

Small device. Clearer skin insights.

DermaSense combines simple sensors with practical skincare education to help you understand your skin.

What it measures

Four useful insights.

Moisture

Learn whether your skin may need more hydration.

pH

Understand whether your skin is within a balanced range.

Oiliness

Observe surface oil levels and possible skin patterns.

Ingredient Guidance

Connect your readings with educational suggestions.

What's in the box?

  • DermaSense measurement device
  • Moisture sensing component
  • pH measurement probe
  • Oiliness measurement materials
  • LCD display
  • Basic calibration guide
