The oil estimate

Why blotting paper?

Oiliness can be estimated by comparing clean blotting paper with paper that has collected oil from the skin.

The BH1750 light sensor observes changes in reflected light between the clean and oil-exposed samples.

Making sense of it

What do the readings mean?

Lower moisture

Your skin may benefit from a gentle, hydrating routine.

Higher oiliness

Lightweight, non-comedogenic products may be useful to explore.

Unbalanced pH

A gentle cleanser and barrier-focused routine may help.
