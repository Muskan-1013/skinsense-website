Our story

Science should feel accessible.

SkinSense was created to make skin analysis more affordable, understandable, and practical.

Many people choose skincare products based on assumptions, trends, or confusing advice.

SkinSense aims to offer a simpler starting point by combining sensors, measurement, and clear educational information.

Our mission is affordable, science-backed skin analysis for everyone.

What guides us

Our values.

Science-backed

We explain how readings are collected and calibrated.

Accessible

We focus on affordable components and simple experiences.

Transparent

We communicate limitations instead of overstating accuracy.
