What to explore

Ingredient suggestions.

For dry skin

Explore humectants, ceramides, and gentle moisturizers.

For oily skin

Explore lightweight hydration and non-comedogenic formulas.

For combination skin

Consider using different products for different areas.

For normal skin

Focus on maintaining balance with a simple routine.

Remember

Repeated measurements are more useful than one isolated result. Skin changes with weather, products, stress, and daily habits.
